# Covered & Stuffed Upholstery: website mockup

Mockup for **Covered & Stuffed Upholstery**, Roleystone WA. They have no website of their own.

- Mobile: 0438 963 884 (ask for Lisa)
- Email on their business card: covered.stuffed@hotmail.com
- Email on Yellow Pages: covered.stuffed@gmail.com
- Workshop: 189 Urch Road, Roleystone WA 6111
- ABN: 61 390 719 400

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step. Open `index.html` to view it.

## Pages

One page: `index.html`. There isn't enough real content for more, and five photos don't need a gallery page.

Their own photos lead. The hero is their striped armchair and green ottoman, pinned up like photo prints on the blue-grey iron wall of their workshop. Below that come four finished pieces (tap one to see it big), what they do, their Google review, their red door ("Look for the red door", with directions), and contact. Blue, red and ivory come from their logo and card.

On the mockup: tap to call, a "Text a photo" button, a photo viewer, an enquiry form and directions.

## Pitch points (checked 24 and 25 Nov 2026, the starred ones again on 26 Nov)

- **No website button on Google.*** Their Google listing has no website, and Google shows "Add website" in its place.
- **"Visit Website" goes nowhere useful.** On Yellow Pages and Whereis it opens their TrueLocal listing. On TrueLocal it links back to the same page.
- **On a phone, Yellow Pages only shows (08) 6271 4430,** not the mobile. Checked as a phone on 25 Nov.
- **The hours don't match.** Google says Mon to Thu 9 to 4 and Fri 9 to 2. Yellow Pages, TrueLocal and Whereis say Mon to Fri 9 to 6.
- **The emails don't match.*** The card in their Google photos says Hotmail. Yellow Pages says Gmail.
- **Yellow Pages spells the suburb "Roley stone".***
- **Good photos, and nowhere of their own to show them.*** They put six photos on Google this month. Facebook shows none to the public. The mockup shows what their own website could look like with them.

### Don't say

- **"Nobody can find you online."** They're on Google, Yellow Pages, TrueLocal, Whereis, MapQuest, Yelp, StartLocal and Facebook.
- **"You have no photos", "no reviews" or "no logo".** Google has six photos and one 5-star review. They have a logo.
- **"Yellow Pages doesn't show your mobile."** On a computer it's in their ad picture, and some searches show it.
- **"The Yellow Pages number is wrong."** It's probably call tracking, and may well reach them.
- **"You're paying for Yellow Pages."** One search listed them as "Sponsored", but I don't know what they pay.
- **"Your Facebook is empty."** It shows no posts or photos when logged out, but may have some when logged in.
- **"We took these photos."** They're theirs, from their Google listing. Say so, and that we'd check with them before using them on a real site.

## Confirm with the owner

- **Photos.** The mockup uses five of the six photos their own account posted on Google. Check she's happy for them to go on the real site, and ask for the originals plus more work, especially before-and-afters.
- **Captions.** They only describe what's in each photo ("Armchair in a bold stripe", "Deep-buttoned ottoman"). They don't name fabrics or say whose piece it was. Check the striped piece is an armchair, not a small sofa.
- **Commercial work.** Two other businesses' Instagram posts credit "Covered & Stuffed Upholstery" for cushions in fit-outs (WA Prestige Cabinets in Armadale, and Rolling Stone Group). Ask if that's them, and whether they have their own photos of those jobs. Those posts' photos aren't theirs, so they're not used.
- **Logo file.** `logo.svg` is a redraw of the logo on their business card. The biggest copy online is 160px wide on Yellow Pages. Their card photo on Google shows it bigger, but it's a photo of a printed card and a bit soft. Ask for the original.
- **Email.** The mockup and form use the one on the card. If the other is right, change it in `index.html` (six places).
- **Hours.** The mockup says "Weekdays. They are flexible, so check before you come out", because the listings disagree.
- **Address.** It's on their card, Facebook and Google, so the mockup shows it. Check they want it there.
- **The services, "free estimates", payment types and "wide range of materials"** come from Yellow Pages, and the directory may have written some of it.
- **The review** is their one Google review. Check they're happy to show it.

## Checks

- **They look to be trading.** They added the photos to Google in November 2026, and the door photo was taken on 11 Nov 2026. ABN Lookup has the ABN in postcode 6112, not 6111, last updated in 2014.
- I found no other website. No one was contacted, and nothing was logged in to.
- Tested on 26 Nov 2026 at 320, 375, 414, 768 and 1280px in light and dark: the skill's audit found 0 problems, and I looked at screenshots at every width. Menu, photo viewer (arrows, swipe, Escape, tap outside, page behind doesn't scroll), theme toggle, form, JavaScript off and blocked storage all checked. Not tested on a real phone or with reduced motion switched on (the code handles it, but I couldn't switch it on in the browser).
- Pages are marked `noindex` so Google doesn't list the mockup. Remove that line at launch. The preview card (og: tags) assumes the site lives at `https://dante1243.github.io/covered-and-stuffed/`.
- The form opens the visitor's own email app. Connect a form service at launch.
